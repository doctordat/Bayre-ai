# Vietnamese Search Intent Research: Pre-Booking Decisions

_Date: 2026-09-02_

## 1. Booking Lead Time ("Nên đặt vé máy bay trước bao lâu?")
- **Intent**: Users want to know the optimal booking window for domestic and international flights.
- **PAA/SERP signals**: "Có thể đặt vé trước 1 tháng không?", "Thời gian giữ vé là bao lâu?", "Mua vé thời điểm nào rẻ nhất?".
- **Action**: Create a guide emphasizing flexibility, route-dependent timing, and avoiding fixed "magic" windows.

## 2. One-way vs Round-trip ("Vé một chiều hay khứ hồi")
- **Intent**: Understanding the cost and flexibility trade-offs between ticket types.
- **Action**: Guide comparing baggage, flexibility, and multi-city itineraries.

## 3. Name Errors ("Sai tên trên vé máy bay")
- **Intent**: Panic/resolution when passenger names have typos.
- **PAA/SERP signals**: "Đảo tên có sao không?", "Phí đổi tên".
- **Action**: Provide a calm, step-by-step checklist to verify rules with the specific airline/partner without promising free fixes.

## 4. Online Check-in & Airport Timing ("Check-in online cần giấy tờ gì")
- **Intent**: Logistics and document prep for flights.
- **Action**: Emphasize that online check-in doesn't bypass ID checks or baggage drop lines. Provide a pre-departure timeline.

## 5. Flight Time Selection ("Chọn giờ bay thế nào để tổng chi phí thấp hơn")
- **Intent**: Balancing cheap early/late hours vs. total trip convenience.
- **Action**: Guide on calculating door-to-door time and hidden costs (late-night taxi, extra hotel nights) of cheap red-eye flights.
